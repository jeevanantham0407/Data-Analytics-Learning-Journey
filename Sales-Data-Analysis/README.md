# Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data using MySQL, Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib.
The objective is to understand sales performance across products, categories,
regions, salespersons, and months.

## Technologies Used

- MySQL
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- mysql-connector-python

## Dataset

The dataset contains 20 sales records with the following columns:

- OrderID
- OrderDate
- Product
- Category
- Region
- Salesperson
- Quantity
- Sales

## Analysis Performed

- Overall sales analysis
- Category-wise sales
- Region-wise sales
- Salesperson-wise sales
- Product-wise sales
- Quantity-wise product analysis
- Average sales by region
- Monthly sales analysis

## Key Business Insights

1. The company generated total sales of ₹864,500 from 20 orders.

2. Electronics was the highest-performing category, generating ₹764,000
   in sales.

3. Mumbai was the top-performing region with total sales of ₹286,000.

4. Sneha was the highest-performing salesperson with sales of ₹286,000.

5. Laptop generated the highest revenue among products with ₹420,000.

6. Mouse had the highest sales quantity with 29 units sold.

7. April recorded the highest monthly sales of ₹236,000.

8. Mumbai also had the highest average sales per order at ₹57,200.

9. Accessories generated significantly lower revenue than Electronics,
   indicating an opportunity to improve accessory sales.

10. The difference between the highest-revenue product and highest-quantity
    product shows that sales volume and revenue are not always the same.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analysis workflow using MySQL,
Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib. The analysis identifies important sales
patterns and business insights that can support better decision-making
related to products, regions, categories, and sales performance.
